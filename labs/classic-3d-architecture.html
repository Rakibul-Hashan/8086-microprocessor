Key ideas

• The 8086 is split into two units that work in parallel: BIU (fetches) and EU (executes).

• Overlapping fetch and execute is pipelining; the 6-byte queue keeps the EU fed.

• Physical address (20-bit) = Segment × 10H + Offset → 1 MB address space.

• A jump or call flushes the queue because the prefetched bytes are no longer valid.
